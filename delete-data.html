Request Data Deletion

For Robot GO! and Momodoku, from Hypercube Games, LLC · Effective date: September 8, 2026

The short version: our games have no accounts and no servers of ours. Everything you build up lives on your device, and each app can erase all of it from its own Settings screen — no request needed, no waiting.

Robot GO! — delete from inside the app

This is the fastest and most complete option, and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Robot GO!.
  2. Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete My Data.
  4. Confirm when prompted. This cannot be undone.
DataWhat happens
Puzzle progress, completed levels, chapter positionErased from your device immediately
Settings, hints, hearts, streaks, daily-challenge historyErased from your device immediately
Anonymous analytics identifierDiscarded and replaced with a new random one, so past activity can no longer be associated with your device
Advertising consentWithdrawn — ads are served non-personalized from that point on. In the EEA and UK the consent form is reset and shown again next launch
"Remove Ads" purchaseKept. This is held by Apple or Google against your store account, not by us, so that you can restore a purchase you paid for. See below

Momodoku — delete from inside the app

  1. Open Momodoku.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Reset progress.
  4. Confirm when prompted. This cannot be undone.

This erases your puzzle progress, streaks, race standing, settings, and power-up charges from your device. Momodoku has no in-app purchases, so there is no purchase record to keep. To clear the advertising identifier held by our ad partners, reset it in your device settings as described below.

Or just uninstall

For either game, deleting the app from your device removes everything it saved. Because none of it is stored on our servers, uninstalling erases your progress along with it.

What we keep, and for how long

We hold no accounts, no names, no email addresses, and no profiles. There is nothing on our side keyed to you as a person to delete.

Two things are outside our control and worth knowing about:

  • Your purchase record (Robot GO! only). If you bought "Remove Ads," Apple or Google records that against your store account so it can be restored on a new device. We can't delete that, and deleting it would cost you the purchase. To remove a purchase record, contact Apple Support or Google Play Support.
  • Advertising identifiers. Our advertising partners hold the device advertising ID under their own retention policies. You can reset or clear that identifier yourself at any time — on iOS via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, on Android via Settings → Google → Ads. Their policies: Google, AppLovin, Unity, RevenueCat.

Robot GO! records anonymous gameplay events (for example "level 12 completed") under a random identifier held by our analytics provider, Amplitude, under their retention policy. These events are not linked to your identity, and using Delete My Data replaces that identifier so earlier events can no longer be tied to your device. Momodoku has no analytics at all.
