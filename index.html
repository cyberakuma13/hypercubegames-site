The studio

Hypercube Games is a small independent studio in Dublin, California, working across tabletop and digital. We've been at this since 2011, when our first mobile game went out on the App Store.

Everything here is made in-house — design, art direction, code, and the endless playtesting in between. We like games with a clean rule you can learn in a minute and consequences you're still thinking about an hour later.
