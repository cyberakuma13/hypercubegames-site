Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

The short version: our games have no accounts, never ask for your name or email, and your progress lives on your device.

This website

hypercubegames.com is a static site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and collects nothing about you. If you email us, we keep that email so we can reply to it.

Lost Robot (mobile game)

The sections below cover the Lost Robot app specifically.

Information the app stores on your device

Your puzzle progress, settings, hints, hearts, and daily-challenge history are saved locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional "watch an ad" rewards. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location inferred from IP address, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads.

  • In the European Economic Area and UK, a consent form is shown before any ad is requested, as required by law. You can decline personalized ads.
  • On iOS, the app asks for tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized using cross-app identifiers.
  • California residents can turn on "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" in the app's Settings, which makes every ad request non-personalized.

Google's own privacy policy applies to AdMob's processing: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases (RevenueCat / Apple / Google)

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play — the app never sees your payment details. RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and your purchase state so the purchase can be restored on reinstall. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Analytics (Amplitude)

The app records anonymous gameplay events (for example "level 12 completed" or "hint used") under a random identifier so we can find confusing levels and fix bugs. These events are not linked to your identity, and we do not collect names, emails, contacts, or precise location. Amplitude's privacy policy: amplitude.com/privacy

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app schedules local reminders (for example, your daily-challenge streak). These are generated on your device; no push server is involved.

Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children. Ads are requested with Google's "not child-directed" setting. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Personalized ads — decline via the consent form (EEA/UK), the iOS tracking prompt, or the "Do Not Sell" toggle in Settings.
  • Delete your data — the app's Settings include "Delete My Data," which wipes local progress and resets the ad-consent state. Identifiers held by Google, RevenueCat, or Amplitude can be reset through your device's advertising-ID settings or by contacting us.
  • Questions or requests — email support@hypercubegames.com and we will help, including with access or deletion requests under GDPR or CCPA.

Changes
